How Hamaya works

One governed path from idea to finished video.

Every screen below is a real capture from Hamaya Studio OS.

01 · Idea

Start in your own words.

Describe what you want to make. Hamaya returns a Brief you can check — what it understood, what you set, and what it left for you to decide. Reading your idea is free: no AI is used and nothing is charged.

Idea → Brief
02 · Story

See the film before anything is generated.

Your story is laid out as a storyboard, shot by shot, so you can shape it first. Nothing is generated until you press a priced button.

Story → Storyboard
03 · Shots

Several Takes. One choice — yours.

Each shot can hold several IMAGE Takes. Viewing a Take never selects it. A Take enters your film only when you press Use Take.

Shots → IMAGE Takes → Use Take
04 · Inspection

AI inspection that shows its evidence.

Ask AI to inspect an image Take for composition, story requirements and artifacts. Each finding explains itself, the price is shown before you ask, and your selection is never changed for you.

AI IMAGE Inspection
05 · Narration

Narration and voice, directed in place.

Shape and approve the narration, then choose and approve the voice recording — inside the same workflow, not in another tool.

Narration → Voice
06 · Finished Video

Finalize, render, finish.

Finalize shows exactly which Takes, narration and pacing your video will use — and what it costs — before anything renders. Then Hamaya renders the finished vertical video.

Finalize → Render → Finished Video

Why Hamaya

AI does the making. You keep the authority.

Hamaya is built around the decisions that make work yours — and makes each one visible.

You are the final authority

AI proposes; you decide. Nothing is selected, approved or rendered on your behalf.

Explicit Take selection

Viewing is not choosing. A Take enters your film only when you press Use Take.

Inspectable AI output

AI inspection explains itself: every finding comes with the evidence behind it.

Transparent spend

Every AI action shows its credit price before you press it, and Finalize shows the render cost up front.

Provenance

A finished video records what it was made from: the selected Takes, the approved narration and the voice used.

A governed workflow

Brief, story, shots, inspection, narration and finalize run in one ordered path — nothing important is skipped or done silently.

Spend transparency

Know what your video will use — and cost — before it renders.

Finalize is the last stop before rendering. It lists everything the video will be made from and states the cost plainly.

  • Every selected Take, shot by shot
  • Whether approved narration is included
  • Pacing for each shot, under your control
  • The credit cost, shown before you press render

Where we're going

Deeper creator control is in development.

Today Hamaya finishes image-based stories with narration. We are building toward more precise control: refining a single detail of a Take, comparing versions side by side, and inspecting moving images. These are future direction, not part of the product today.
